## Make Friends. Have Fun.

> Unified Social Club is a free club for adults with special needs in Poway. We meet two Fridays a month to play games, make art, sing karaoke, and play sports.

## What We Do

> Every club date is free. Aides, family members, and service animals are welcome.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>🃏 Board and Card Games</strong><p>Games that are easy to learn and fun to play with friends.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>🎨 Arts and Crafts</strong><p>Painting, crafts, and more. There is no wrong way to make art.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>🎤 Karaoke</strong><p>Sing your favorite songs, or cheer for your friends.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell"><strong>🏀 Sports</strong><p>Play games with new friends and old friends.</p></div>
</div>
