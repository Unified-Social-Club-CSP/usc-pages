> On Monday, November 2, Team Triple T (Kashyap, Chetan, and Aaditya) presented Unified Social Club at Night at the Museum. It went great: many adults and kids stopped by, tried our games, and left microblog notes on our site.

## What we showed

- **Who we serve.** Unified Social Club is a free club in Poway where adults with special needs make friends through games, arts and crafts, karaoke, and sports. We opened by talking about the club before any demo.
- **Three club games.** Club Bingo (Kashyap), Club Trivia Live (Chetan), and Club Jeopardy (Aaditya), each kept to a short demo.
- **Built for every member.** Big print, text-size buttons, picture answers, read-aloud, and Nod to select, which lets someone play with a head nod instead of a mouse.
- **Built on OCS.** Our capstone page was on one screen and the live demo on the other, so we could hand conversations to each other when a line formed.

## Who came

Many adults and kids came to our table and saw our presentation. Talking with families, students, and community members was the best part of the night.

## What visitors left us

Visitors posted microblog notes on our site during the event. Those notes are our first real feedback from people outside our class, and we are going through them as a team.

## What's next

- Turn the microblog feedback into issues on our [Kanban board](https://github.com/orgs/Unified-Social-Club-CSP/projects/1).
- Keep building each game: live Trivia rooms, server-checked Bingo wins, and volunteer-made Jeopardy boards.
- Reach out to Unified Social Club with what we learned and ask what members want next.
