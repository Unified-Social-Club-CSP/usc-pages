## The Club

> A social club for adults with special needs, started by families in Poway.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>How It Started</strong>
        <p>The club grew out of Parent Advocates for Special Services (PASS), started in 1997 by two parents of sons with special needs. When their children became adults, families wanted a social club for them.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent">
        <strong>Who It Is For</strong>
        <p>Adults 18 and older with special needs. Aides, family members, and service animals are welcome.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Where</strong>
        <p>Mickey Cafagna Community Center<br>13094 Civic Center Dr., Poway, CA 92064</p>
        <a class="ocs__btn large accent" href="https://www.unifiedsocialclub.com/blank-2">Their About Page</a>
    </div>
</div>
