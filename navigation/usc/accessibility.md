## How Nod to Select Works

> Four steps. You only need a camera on your computer or tablet.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-4">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>1. Turn it on</strong><p>Press <strong>Nod to Select</strong> at the top of any page. Your browser asks to use the camera.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>2. Hold still</strong><p>The camera learns how you sit when you're resting.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>3. Watch the outline</strong><p>It moves slowly from button to button in the game.</p></div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--accent"><strong>4. Nod to press</strong><p>Chin down, then back up. The outlined button is pressed for you.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="ocs__links">

</div>
